Subscribing to the calendar

  1. Open the tool and select your year and elective courses (Opção I–IV) so the schedule matches what you're actually taking.
  2. Copy the calendar URL it generates — this is a live feed, not a one-off download.
  3. Add it to your calendar app as a "subscription" or "from URL" calendar (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook all support this) so future changes show up automatically.
